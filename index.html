03 — Process

Engineered, not improvised.

My background is software. So every ad I ship is a measurable, debuggable, iterable system.

  1. 01

    Discovery & Diagnostic

    Audit existing creatives, ICP, offer, and competitor hooks. Define the conversion thesis we'll test against.

  2. 02

    Hook Engineering

    3–5 distinct hook angles per concept — pattern interrupt, problem-agitate, social proof, transformation, native.

  3. 03

    Edit & Motion

    Premiere Pro + After Effects. Pacing tuned to platform algo. Captions, SFX, and B-roll built for retention.

  4. 04

    Ship & Iterate

    Multi-aspect delivery. Then we read the data: Hook Rate, Hold Rate, CTR, ROAS — and ship V2 within 72h.
